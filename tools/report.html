Suspected factors (odds ratios)

Read this first. These are associations within your own log, computed the same way as the Megrim app: an odds ratio over migraine-days (2×2 table, Haldane–Anscombe correction, study window = first to last migraine day). Association is not causation. Small samples are noisy, and with many factors tested some will look elevated purely by chance. Treat these as hypotheses to discuss with a clinician — not conclusions. Weather/pressure-change factors are omitted here because full daily pressure history only exists inside the app.

Methods. Descriptive charts are plain counts of logged entries — a tall bar means "more migraines were logged there," nothing more. Suspected factors use the unit of a migraine-day (multi-day migraines cover each day they span); every day in the study window is a baseline day. For each bucket a 2×2 table is built and a Haldane–Anscombe-corrected odds ratio computed (0.5 added to every cell). A factor is shown only if its bucket covers at least 3 migraine-days and its OR exceeds 1.0; the strongest 8 are listed, matching the Megrim app (correlations.dart). Moon phase and daylight are computed astronomically from your home latitude; season is hemisphere-corrected.
